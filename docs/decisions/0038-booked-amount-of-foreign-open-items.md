# 0038 Booked amount of foreign-currency open items
Status: accepted (Phase 6 review B1). Code: `apps/worker/src/exact/sync.ts` (`openItemStmt`, `syncOpenItemsFull`, `withComputedBooked`), `packages/core/src/fx.ts` (`bookedAtRate`), `packages/exact/src/client.ts` (`OPEN_ITEM_LIST_DC_FIELDS`), `apps/worker/src/exact/sync-routes.ts` (settings).

## Problem
The unrealised FX difference needs the booked amount of an open item in the administration currency: `open_items.open_dc_minor` (with `dc_currency`). Only the incremental sync endpoint (`/sync/Cashflow/*`: `AmountDC`, `RateFC`) carries it, and that read is off by default (ADR 0017, same-ID assumption). The nightly full list pass (`read/financial/ReceivablesList` / `PayablesList`) has no such field in its `$select` and used to overwrite the column with NULL on every item, so the "Booked" line and the difference never showed in a default install.

## Decision
1. **Stop the wipe (always on).** The upsert keeps `open_dc_minor` and `dc_currency` when the read carries none, but only while `currency` and `open_minor` are unchanged. When the open amount or the currency changed they are cleared (a stale booked value would be wrong money). A read that carries a value replaces it. A part payment seen only by the nightly list therefore clears the booked amount of the remainder until a sync read or option 3 gives it again; the UI already says "Booked amount not known yet" in that case. `original_minor` and `rate` keep their ADR 0017 rule (kept while the currency is unchanged).
2. **Three ways to fill it**, all admin-controlled and verified live before use:
   - **Option 1: list fields.** The documented fields of `ReceivablesList` / `PayablesList` (ADR 0014 item 3) include no division-currency amount and no rate. `AmountDC` and `RateFC` are a guess copied from the sync resource, so they are NOT requested by default. Setting `exact.list_dc_fields` (admin, `PUT /api/sync/settings {listDcFields}`, audited) adds them to the list `$select`. Exact answering 400 to it (not our gate's own 400s) makes the read repeat once without them and the entity remember it for 7 days (`sync_state.unavailable_until` on the entity's row, like `budgets_unavailable`); a `restart` run or changing the setting forgets it. Other errors are not treated this way. Cost: one extra call per list when it is refused, once a week; none otherwise.
   - **Option 2: a cheap bulk read.** Not built: no known endpoint is cheaper than the sync endpoints, which already exist behind the incremental setting. Turning that setting on after the same-ID check (DEPLOY.md item 9) is the way to get `AmountDC` and `RateFC` from Exact itself.
   - **Option 3: open amount times the booked rate.** `exact.rate_direction` = `base_per_foreign` (division-currency units per 1 unit of the item currency, 0.92 for USD in a EUR division) or `foreign_per_base` (the ECB way, 1.087), unset by default. Unset means a booked amount is never computed. When set, for each foreign-currency item of a list chunk that carries no division-currency amount, the worker computes `open_dc_minor` from the open amount and the rate (the list's own `RateFC`, else the rate stored from an earlier sync read for the same currency), with core `bookedAtRate`: exact BigInt arithmetic, one rounding half away from zero (ADR 0035), the currency scales of both currencies. An item with no rate stays without a booked amount. A stored amount read from Exact for this very open balance is kept in preference to a computed one. One D1 read per chunk, no Exact calls. Set after checking one invoice both ways (`DEPLOY.md` item 17); a wrong direction would book wrong money, so the setting is admin only and audited (`settings.exact_rate_direction`). Changing the direction later does not recompute amounts already stored for unchanged open balances; clear them with a full sync `restart` after correcting the cause (open question below).
3. **Dormant until verified.** With nothing switched on and the incremental read off, the booked amount stays empty and the acceptance criterion "a USD invoice shows Booked and the difference the morning after a full sync" is dormant. The first-live checklist (DEPLOY.md step 10, item 17) says how to switch it on.

## What this does not decide
- Whether Exact's `AmountDC` includes earlier revaluations (open decision 3). Option 3 applies the invoice's booked rate to the remainder; if Exact revalues open items, its `AmountDC` differs and the sync read wins whenever present.
- Credit notes: amounts are signed, `bookedAtRate` is odd in the sign, so a negative open amount gives a negative booked amount.
