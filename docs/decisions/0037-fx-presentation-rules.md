# 0037 FX presentation rules (Phase 6, F3)
Status: accepted. Code: `apps/web/src/{fx.tsx,lib/fx.ts,components/Fx*.tsx,pages/FxRates.tsx}`.
- **What is converted.** Only to show an estimate at today's ECB rate; nothing is booked and no stored figure is replaced. Original amounts stay the primary numbers everywhere. Ledger reports (balance sheet, profit and loss) are untouched: they are already in the administration currency.
- **Base currency** is the selected administration's currency (`DivisionInfo.currency`, read from Exact). EUR is assumed only while no administration is selected, and the screens say so ("... is not known yet, so EUR is assumed"). Conversion is any currency to the base through the EUR cross rate (core `convertMinor`), so USD to GBP is one conversion with one rounding, not USD to EUR to GBP.
- **Never sum raw currencies.** A total in the base currency is the sum of per-currency converted totals: amounts of one currency are added first (exact integers), each currency is converted once, then the results are added. So two items of 0,05 USD give 0,09 EUR (0,10 USD converted once), not 0,10 EUR. A currency with no ECB rate is left out and listed ("Not included: GBP (no ECB rate)"); a total that rests on a missing rate is never shown as complete.
- **Wording.** Converted figures say "about" and are labelled an estimate at today's ECB rates, for information only, with the rate date (the oldest leg of a cross rate). A bank transaction or open item is valued at today's rate, not the rate on the day. A stale feed shows an amber chip ("Rates from 6 Nov, one working day old"); never imported shows "No ECB rates imported yet"; a missing currency shows "No ECB rate for GBP" (not an error). Text carries every state; colour is never the only signal.
- **Booked amounts come from Exact only when known.** The booked base amount is `open_dc_minor`, used only when every item behind the figure has one (`dcComplete`). Otherwise "Booked amount not known yet" (never 0, with "at least" when part is known). The difference is today's value minus booked (ADR 0035, not a gain/loss sign) and is worded by kind: receivable "you would receive X more/less", payable "you would pay X more/less". A credit note flips the words ("refund" for a sales credit note, "get back" for a purchase one) because its amount is negative; the gain/loss label comes from `unrealisedResultMinor`.
- **Aggregates.** A KPI or ageing figure is converted from its summed amount in one step; its difference compares that with the sum of booked amounts. This can differ by a few cents from adding item-by-item conversions (the month-end booking in Phase 12 rounds item by item, ADR 0035). The Overview total and the ageing "Total in EUR at today's ECB rates" row use the same rule; they are not drill-down figures.
- **No floats.** BigInt minor units, core decimal strings, integer chart coordinates (BigInt division). A source scan (`fx-no-float.test.ts`) forbids `parseFloat`, `Number(`, `toFixed` and `Math.round` family in the FX files.
- **Data flow.** One `GET /api/fx/rates` per page load in `FxProvider` (inside the division provider), re-read on focus and every 30 minutes; the previous rates stay on screen until new ones arrive and a failed re-read keeps them. Without rates, the Overview and ageing fall back to Exact's own amount line.
- **Exports.** The ageing Excel gets a "Total in EUR" sheet (exact number text, rate date, estimate note, excluded currencies); print shows the on-screen row.
- **Base currency per figure (review F3).** An item sheet converts into the item's own division currency (`FxAmount base=`), the currency its booked amount is in, so booked and today's value can never be in two currencies. Overview and ageing figures are converted only when the report's administration currency equals the selected one; during a switch they show Exact's own line. While the administration list is loading, nothing is converted to the EUR fall-back (`ready` is false).
- **No layout jump (review F3).** While the first rates read is under way (`pending`), each FX line is replaced by a placeholder line of the same class (`[data-fx-pending]`, hidden from assistive technology and from print): FxAmount and FxDcLine reserve the lines they will show, FxRowLine the row's second line, and the Overview total and Bank total blocks their own height. A failed first read removes the placeholders and shows Exact's own line (Overview, ageing) or "No estimate: the ECB rates could not be read" (sheets).
- **Amounts that cannot be used exactly** (a JSON number that is not a safe integer) are never passed to `BigInt()` (which throws on 1.5 and silently rounds above 2^53): the currency is left out and listed as "amount not usable". A GBP administration without a GBP rate lists the other currencies as "Not included: USD (no ECB rate for GBP)", naming the rate that is really missing.
- **Partly known booked amounts.** When only some items have a booked amount, no difference is shown; the known part is given as "booked for the items Exact has an amount for", not "at least", because credit notes make a partial sum no lower bound.
- **Import code.** `FxRatesResponse.lastImportCode` (optional in the type, always sent by the Worker) carries `fx_import_state.last_error_code`. The FX tab shows it with plain words, for example "failed (rate_not_published): the ECB had not published today's rates by 17:30"; an unknown code is shown as the code, a value that does not look like a code is not shown. Import now refuses a second start while one is in flight (a ref, not only the disabled state), re-reads at 4, 12 and 30 s after a start or a 409, and clears its timers on unmount.
- **Float scan.** `fx-no-float.test.ts` scans every source file named `fx*`/`Fx*` and every file importing the FX modules, so new FX files are covered automatically; template-literal expressions are scanned (the first version blanked them). Non-FX uses in those files are allowed by exact count with a reason.
